We're sharing a review of the evidence on worker retraining programs, coauthored by independent researcher David Roodman and Anthropic's Maxim Massenkoff.

我们发布一份关于工人转岗培训（worker retraining）项目证据的综述，由独立研究员 David Roodman 与 Anthropic 的 Maxim Massenkoff 合著。

Retraining workers is the most popular policy option for mitigating labor market disruption from AI. In this report, the authors investigate whether these programs would work in the face of significant labor market disruption.

对工人进行再培训，是缓解 AI 带来的劳动力市场动荡最受欢迎的政策选项。在本报告中，作者们研究了：面对大规模的劳动力市场动荡，这些项目究竟能否奏效。

The review is part of our Economic Research team's work on AI's effects on the economy. Our Economic Index tracks how AI is being used across occupations and industries. Earlier this year, we published a framework for measuring AI's effects on the labor market and identifying the jobs most likely to be affected. Our Economic Policy Framework sets out possible policy responses, including worker retraining, across a range of scenarios. We want to understand the evidence behind each of these policy responses.

本综述是经济研究团队关于 AI 经济影响工作的一部分。我们的经济指数（Economic Index）追踪 AI 在各职业与各行业中的使用情况；今年早些时候，我们发布了度量 AI 对劳动力市场影响、并识别最可能受影响岗位的框架；我们的经济政策框架（Economic Policy Framework）则在一系列情景下列出了包括工人再培训在内的政策应对选项。我们想弄清楚：每项政策应对背后的证据是什么。

The review draws on 56 randomized US studies, combined in a new meta-analysis, along with experimental evidence from Europe. On average, job training programs produce positive but modest effects: for each person offered a training slot, employment rises by two to three percentage points and earnings by roughly $1,000 a year, against a cost of about $13,000. Counting the added tax revenue and reduced benefit payments, the government recovers more than half of what it spends, and programs roughly break even overall.

本综述纳入了 56 项美国随机实验研究（汇成一项新的元分析），以及来自欧洲的实验证据。平均而言，职业培训项目带来正向但温和的效果：每提供 1 个培训名额，就业率上升 2 到 3 个百分点，年收入增加约 1,000 美元，而成本约为 13,000 美元。把新增税收与减少的福利支出计算在内，政府能收回超过一半的开销，项目整体上大致收支相抵。

A small set of "sector programs"—programs that partner with employers in a high-demand industry and place people directly into jobs in that field—produce gains several times larger. But attempts to replicate them have often failed. The authors conclude that if AI displaces workers at scale, existing retraining programs would likely fall short.

一小部分"行业项目"（sector programs）——与高需求行业的雇主合作、把人直接安置进该领域岗位的项目——产生的收益要大上几倍。但复制它们的尝试往往以失败告终。作者的结论是：如果 AI 大规模取代工人，现有的转岗培训项目很可能力有不逮。

Their central recommendation is to invest now in demonstrating, evaluating, and scaling the most promising programs, including through an effort to rapidly expand a leading program for a specific group of workers and rigorously measure the results. Our Economic Futures Research Fund is designed to fund investigations into questions like these.

他们的核心建议是：现在就投资于展示、评估和规模化最有前景的项目，包括迅速扩展一个面向特定工人群体的领先项目并严格测量其结果。我们的经济未来研究基金（Economic Futures Research Fund）正是为资助这类问题的研究而设立。
