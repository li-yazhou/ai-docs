# 在前沿工作如何塑造对未来的期望（中英对照）

> 原文标题：Working at the frontier shapes expectations for the future
> 原文链接：https://www.anthropic.com/research/expectations-for-the-future

We're sharing two reports: the first covers how AI is changing employment inside Anthropic, and the second looks at the gap between how the public and Anthropic employees expect AI to affect work and the economy.

我们在此发布两份报告：第一份讲 AI 正如何改变 Anthropic 内部的就业形态；第二份考察公众与 Anthropic 员工在「AI 将如何影响工作与经济」预期上的差距。

Anthropic staff and US workers don't seem to have different theories about how AI affects jobs. Instead, they have different experiences of what AI capabilities are. Those different experiences seem closely related to perceptions about what will happen to work in the future.

Anthropic 员工与美国劳动者对「AI 如何影响工作」似乎并无不同的理论——他们不同的是对「AI 能力究竟到了哪一步」的体验。而这些不同的体验，与他们对未来工作走向的判断密切相关。

Anthropic is a company at the frontier of AI adoption, and that adoption has changed the way we hire. We find that over the past year, we're hiring for a smaller share of junior positions, and a smaller share of our employees work in technical roles. At the same time, the rate of hours worked by AI agents has shot up. By March 2026, agents worked more hours than employees. By August, there were six times more agents than employees at Anthropic, and productivity increased.

Anthropic 是一家站在 AI 采用最前沿的公司，这种采用已经改变了我们的招聘方式。我们发现：过去一年里，初级岗位在招聘中的占比在缩小，员工中从事技术岗位的比例也在缩小。与此同时，AI agent 的工时速率急剧上升：到 2026 年 3 月，agent 的工时已超过员工；到 8 月，Anthropic 内 agent 数量已是员工的六倍，而生产率在提高。

That's led to Anthropic producing more code per person, but that's a notoriously imperfect measure. We see signs of increased productivity elsewhere: we've closed more sales per sales representative, and processed more contracts. When our researchers follow the same employee over time, they find the employee's output rises as their adoption of AI tools increases.

其结果是 Anthropic 人均产出更多代码——不过这是出了名的不完美度量。我们在别处也看到了生产率提升的迹象：每位销售代表成交更多销售、处理更多合同。当研究者对同一名员工做时间纵向跟踪时，发现随着其 AI 工具采用度的提高，个人产出也在上升。

All this acceleration means that, increasingly, the bottleneck is human attention. As a result, employees spend more time building ways to better monitor work their agents are doing.

这一连串加速意味着：瓶颈日益变成人类的注意力。于是员工把更多时间花在搭建「更好监控 agent 所做工作」的手段上。

Anthropic could be a leading indicator of how AI will shift work organization, hiring, and labor demand across the economy. An AI lab is different from more traditional businesses in how it adopts AI into its work, but research findings about our own workplace may still be instructive about broader transformations, including dynamics for future AI-native companies.

对于 AI 将如何在整个经济中改变工作组织、招聘与劳动力需求，Anthropic 或许是一个先行指标。AI 实验室在把 AI 引入自身工作方面与传统企业不同，但关于我们自己职场的研究发现，对更广泛的转型——包括未来 AI 原生公司的动态——可能仍有借鉴意义。

Because Anthropic employees experience AI's effect on work so differently, it stands to reason that they might have different predictions about AI's effect on the future of work. That's what our second report investigates. We surveyed Anthropic employees, and compared them to an August survey of almost 11,000 US adults, fielded by Morning Consult and weighted to be representative of the adult population.

正因为 Anthropic 员工对「AI 影响工作」的体验如此不同，他们对「AI 将如何影响未来工作」的预测也可能不同——这正是第二份报告要考察的。我们调查了 Anthropic 员工，并与 Morning Consult 于 8 月执行、经加权以代表成年人口的近 1.1 万名美国成年人调查做了对比。

The public expects AI capabilities to advance rapidly in the coming years; Anthropic staff tend to expect even faster progress. There are a number of questions with big gaps between staff and the public. For example, 39% of US adults—and 91% of staff—expect AI will be able to make a Nobel-level discovery by 2030. More than half of Anthropic staff expect AI to be able to do at least 80% of their current work by 2030; only 5% of Americans expect AI to be that capable.

公众预期 AI 能力将在未来几年快速进步；Anthropic 员工往往预期得更快。不少问题上员工与公众之间存在巨大差距：例如，39% 的美国成年人——以及 91% 的员工——预期 AI 到 2030 年能做出诺贝尔级的发现。超过一半的 Anthropic 员工预期 AI 到 2030 年能完成其当前工作的至少 80%；而预期 AI 有此能力的美国人只有 5%。

That said, there's considerable variation in both groups: 2 out of 10 Anthropic staff have expectations similar to those of the typical US adult. And Anthropic staff and the public also agree on some key questions. In each bucket, people who expect AI to do comparably more of their own work anticipate similar increases in unemployment. In other words, both the public and Anthropic staff have similar economic models of how AI affects the labor market, but ultimately their personal experiences with AI lead them to different expectations for the economy of the future.

话虽如此，两个群体内部差异都不小：每 10 名 Anthropic 员工中就有 2 人的期望与普通美国成年人相近。而且在一些关键问题上，员工与公众的判断是一致的：在各个分组里，预期 AI 能完成自己工作中更大比例的人，都会预期失业率出现相近幅度的上升。换句话说，公众与 Anthropic 员工对「AI 如何影响劳动力市场」持有相似的经济学模型；但归根结底，他们与 AI 的个人体验不同，最终导向了对未来经济的不同期望。
