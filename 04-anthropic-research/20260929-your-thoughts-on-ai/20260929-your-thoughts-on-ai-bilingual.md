We're launching a new study using Anthropic Interviewer to learn from your experiences with AI, and we'd like you to participate. After you finish, you can decide to make your interview public, so that anyone, not just Anthropic, can read and learn from it. You can participate here.

我们正在启动一项新研究，使用 Anthropic Interviewer 了解你与 AI 相处的经历，并希望你能参与。完成后，你可以选择把访谈公开，让任何人——而不仅是 Anthropic——都能阅读并从中学习。你可以在原文链接处参与。

We are at a pivotal moment in the development of AI, as its growing capabilities mean it becomes potentially more useful and more dangerous. Frontier AI is rapidly accelerating discoveries in science and medicine, while at the same time the cost of its misuse grows more consequential — a single security incident can cause far more damage than it did a year ago.

AI 的发展正处在一个关键时刻：能力不断增长，意味着它潜在地更有用、也更危险。前沿 AI 正在加速科学与医学的发现，与此同时滥用的代价也愈发严重——同一次安全事件，如今造成的损害可能远超一年前。

How to weigh these benefits and risks shouldn't be left to AI companies alone. Your voice can help guide us, other AI labs, and policymakers as we navigate this uncertain future together. Our big questions are:

如何权衡这些收益与风险，不应只交给 AI 公司。在共同穿越这个不确定未来的过程中，你的声音可以帮助指引我们、其他 AI 实验室与政策制定者。我们的核心问题是：

- What are your most meaningful experiences with AI, both positive and negative?

  你与 AI 最有意义的经历是什么——正面的和负面的？

- Is there anything about how the world works (like work, school, healthcare, or government) that you'd like AI to help change?

  关于世界运转的方式（比如工作、学校、医疗或政府），你希望 AI 帮助改变什么？

- What do you want from the companies developing AI?

  你想从开发 AI 的公司那里得到什么？

This project builds on a similar study conducted last December, in which 81,000 people told us their hopes and worries about AI. That study shaped the Anthropic Institute's agenda, was presented at the World Economic Forum to international leaders and decision-makers, and continues to guide our ongoing Societal Impacts and Economics research.

该项目承接去年 12 月的一项类似研究——81,000 人向我们讲述了他们对 AI 的期望与担忧。那项研究塑造了 Anthropic Institute 的议程，在世界经济论坛上向各国领导人与决策者做过展示，并持续指引我们的社会影响与经济研究。

AI and Anthropic have changed a lot since then. AI has improved, more people use Claude, and people's feelings toward the technology have shifted. For the previous study, only the high-level results and a small number of quotes were made public. This time, we want your thoughts to be made widely available so that anyone can learn from them.

此后 AI 与 Anthropic 都变化很大：AI 进步了，更多人用上 Claude，人们对这项技术的感受也在转变。上一项研究只公开了高层结果与少量引语；这一次，我们希望你的想法能被广泛获取，让任何人都能从中学习。

We've written a FAQ so you can make an informed decision about whether to make your interview public and what to share. You can choose to participate here.

我们准备了一份 FAQ，帮助你在知情的前提下决定是否公开访谈、分享什么。你可以在原文链接处选择参与。

## FAQ

### 访谈的内容是什么？（What's the interview about?）

The interview asks about your experiences with AI and what you want from it. We'll ask about the moments with AI that have mattered most to you, both positive and negative, whether there's anything about how the world works that you'd like AI to help change, and what you want from the companies developing AI, including Anthropic. There are no right answers; we want to hear about your experiences in your own words… And we don't want to give too much away before you take it!

访谈问的是你与 AI 的经历、以及你想从 AI 得到什么。我们会问：与 AI 相处时对你最重要的时刻（无论正负）、关于世界运转方式你希望 AI 帮助改变什么、以及你想从 AI 开发公司（包括 Anthropic）那里得到什么。没有标准答案；我们想听你用自己的话讲述你的经历……在你参与之前，我们也不想剧透太多！

### 我必须公开我的访谈吗？（Do I have to share my interview publicly?）

No. It's optional.

不必，这是可选项。

### 如果我选择公开，会发布什么？（What gets published if I make my interview public?）

We will publish your complete interview and the country associated with your conversation. We will not include your Claude account information, such as name or email address.

我们将发布你的完整访谈以及对话关联的国家。不会包含你的 Claude 账号信息，如姓名或邮箱。

### 公开我的访谈有什么好处？（What are the benefits to publicly sharing my interview?）

Your perspective will be heard not just by us, but by anyone interested in studying and improving AI. We want anyone to be able to study how AI is impacting people and how AI development should progress.

你的视角将不仅被我们听到，也被所有对研究并改进 AI 感兴趣的人听到。我们希望任何人都能研究 AI 正如何影响人们、AI 发展应如何推进。

Researchers and policymakers around the world can read interviews in their own languages, read responses shared from different regions, and ask questions we didn't think to ask. Many bring expertise we don't have on staff, and they may have insights we didn't think to look for. Anyone can check whether our published findings actually reflect what participants said.

世界各地的研究者与政策制定者可以用自己的语言阅读访谈、看到来自不同地区的回答，并提出我们没想到要问的问题。他们中许多人具备我们没有的专长，可能带来我们没想到去寻找的洞见。任何人都可以核验我们发表的发现是否真实反映了参与者所说。

Your interview also becomes part of the public record of this moment in AI, open to future historians, journalists, and researchers, and can inform how society responds to this moment. That record is also hard for AI companies and others shaping AI development to ignore. If many participants say companies like Anthropic should do something differently, that language stays public where anyone can reference it.

你的访谈也将成为 AI 此刻公共记录的一部分，向未来的历史学家、记者与研究者开放，并可启发社会如何回应这个时刻。这份记录也是 AI 公司和其他塑造 AI 发展的力量难以忽视的。如果许多参与者都说 Anthropic 这样的公司应该改变做法，这些话就会留在公开处，任何人都能引用。

### 公开我的访谈有什么风险？（What are the risks to publicly sharing my interview?）

While we won't publish your Claude account information, such as your name or email address, we encourage participants to consider that anyone can read a public interview. This includes employers, friends and relatives, as well as governments and other companies. Depending on what a participant shares, others may be able to link the participant back to their interview. Details that seem harmless on their own, such as age, job, city, or where you studied, can make it easier to identify you. We don't ask for these details in the interview and encourage participants to avoid sharing them.

虽然我们不会发布你的 Claude 账号信息（如姓名或邮箱），但我们提醒参与者：公开访谈任何人都能读到——包括雇主、亲友，也有政府和其他公司。视参与者分享的内容而定，他人也许能把访谈与本人对应起来。诸如年龄、职业、城市、就读学校等看似无害的细节，都可能让人更容易认出你。访谈中我们不会询问这些细节，也建议参与者避免分享。

Once public, an interview could be used in ways a participant does not intend, including by scammers and other people who may seek to misuse personal information. We can remove our copy of your interview on request, but we can't delete what people have already saved. For that reason, treat your decision to make your interview public as permanent.

一旦公开，访谈就可能被以参与者未曾打算的方式使用，包括骗子和其他可能滥用个人信息的人。我们可以应请求删除我们保存的副本，但无法删除别人已经存下的内容。因此，请把「公开访谈」的决定当作不可撤回的决定。

### 有人能从我的公开访谈中认出我吗？（Could someone identify me from my public interview?）

We won't attach your name or account information, but we don't edit interviews, so we can't guarantee you won't be re-identified from what you say. Someone could use AI to combine small details, like employers or past projects, to work out who a participant is. Researchers have shown this can work with interviews from Anthropic Interviewer.

我们不会附上你的姓名或账号信息，但我们不编辑访谈，因此无法保证你不会因所说内容被再次识别。有人可以用 AI 把雇主、过往项目等细碎信息拼起来推断参与者是谁。研究者已证明这种事在 Anthropic Interviewer 的访谈上是可行的。

The best protection is to not share information that could identify you or others. During the interview, we show examples of information you shouldn't share, and you review your full interview before deciding whether to make it public.

最好的保护是不分享可能识别你或他人的信息。访谈过程中我们会展示不应分享的信息示例；在决定是否公开之前，你可以通读自己的完整访谈。

### 我该如何决定是否公开访谈？（How should I decide whether to publicly share my interview?）

Before the interview, we explain what making an interview public involves. We tell participants that we will not include their Claude account information in the public release, but that others may be able to identify them from what they share. We also explain that making an interview public means anyone can read, save, and use it. We can remove our copy on request, but we can't delete what people have already saved.

访谈开始前，我们会说明公开访谈意味着什么：我们不会在公开版本中包含 Claude 账号信息，但他人可能从你分享的内容中识别你；公开意味着任何人都能阅读、保存与使用它；我们可以应请求删除自己的副本，但删不掉别人已保存的内容。

During the interview, you will see examples of the types of sensitive information we advise not to share. After the interview, you can reread what you said before deciding whether to share it publicly. You will be reminded of both the benefits and risks, and if you choose to share, we will confirm your decision one last time.

访谈过程中，你会看到我们建议不要分享的敏感信息类型示例。访谈结束后，你可以重读自己所说的内容再决定是否公开。我们会再次提示收益与风险；如果你选择公开，我们会最后确认一次你的决定。

### 如果我不公开我的访谈，会怎样？（What happens if I don't make my interview public?）

We'll still analyze it as part of our Societal Impacts research, but your full interview won't be published. We may still publish and share aggregated or de-identified findings resulting from your interview. If you don't want us to analyze it either, you can end the interview early (see "Can I decide to end the interview early and delete the record?" below for more detail).

我们仍会把它作为社会影响研究的一部分进行分析，但不会发布你的完整访谈。由你的访谈得出的聚合或去标识化发现仍可能被发布与分享。如果你也不希望被分析，可以提前结束访谈（详见下文「我可以提前结束访谈并删除记录吗？」）。

### 如果我公开了访谈，可以反悔吗？（If I make my interview publicly available, can I change my mind?）

Not fully. Once your interview is public, anyone can read, save, and use it. We can remove our copy of your interview on request, but we can't delete what people have already saved. Treat the decision as permanent. Learn more.

不能完全反悔。访谈一旦公开，任何人都可以阅读、保存与使用。我们可以应请求删除自己保存的副本，但删不掉别人已保存的内容。请把这个决定当作永久的。更多信息见原文。

### 发布之前你们会编辑我的访谈吗？（Will you edit my interview before publishing it?）

No, we don't plan to edit interviews before publishing them. Once the study is complete, we'll do a final review and may remove interviews we don't believe would be responsible to publish, such as interviews with confidential information or ones that violate our Usage Policy or Terms of Service.

不会，我们无意在发布前编辑访谈。研究完成后我们会做最终审查，可能移除我们认为不宜发布的访谈——例如含机密信息的，或违反我们的使用政策或服务条款的。

### Anthropic 会如何使用我的访谈？（How will Anthropic use my interview?）

We'll analyze interviews as part of our Societal Impacts research, share our findings publicly, and use the results to inform how we develop our models and products. Learn more.

我们会把访谈作为社会影响研究的一部分进行分析、公开发布发现，并以此指导我们的模型与产品开发。更多信息见原文。

### 我可以提前结束访谈并删除记录吗？（Can I decide to end the interview early and delete the record?）

Yes, you can stop at any time by clicking the "End interview" button. If you end early, your partial interview will be permanently deleted, and won't be analyzed or published, including by Anthropic.

可以。点击「结束访谈」按钮即可随时停止。提前结束时，你的不完整访谈将被永久删除，不会被分析或发布——包括 Anthropic 在内。

### 谁可以参与？需要多久？（Who can take the interview and how long does it take?）

This study will run from September 29 to October 6, 2026, and is open to Free, Pro, and Max users on Claude and Claude Code whose accounts are at least two weeks old. Each interview takes roughly 15 minutes and is conducted by Anthropic Interviewer, an AI that asks you questions.

本研究将于 2026 年 9 月 29 日至 10 月 6 日进行，面向 Claude 与 Claude Code 上账号满两周的 Free、Pro 与 Max 用户。每次访谈约 15 分钟，由 Anthropic Interviewer——一个向你提问的 AI——主持。

We updated the questions, the study is open on more Claude products, and for the first time you can choose to make your interview public.

我们更新了问题，研究扩展到更多 Claude 产品，而且首次允许你选择公开访谈。

### 访谈将于何时何地发布？（When and where will the interviews be published?）

The interviews will be published online once we have completed the study and our initial analysis.

研究完成并完成初步分析后，访谈将在线上发布。

### 这项研究有什么局限？（What are the limitations of this study?）

Participants in this study will all be people who use Claude, which is not a representative sample of the public. Published interviews are a further subset, since people who opt in for public release may differ from those who don't. And as with any interview, the questions we ask shape what people share. Even so, nothing like this has been public before. We believe it can significantly advance the study of how AI is changing people's lives.

本研究的参与者都是 Claude 用户，并非公众的代表性样本。公开的访谈是进一步的子集——选择公开的人可能与不选择的人不同。而且与任何访谈一样，我们问的问题会塑造人们分享的内容。即便如此，这样的事从未公开过。我们相信它能显著推进「AI 正如何改变人们的生活」的研究。
